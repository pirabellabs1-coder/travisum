Documents to provide

Documents and procedures.

Passport
  • — Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months after the date of your visa application.
  • — It must also contain at least 2 blank pages.
Two identity photos
  • — These must be in colour, taken against a plain light background, in 35x45mm format and printed on photo paper (as for your identity card or passport).
  • — You can check whether your photos meet the required standards using the following document: Photo requirements
Two official visa application forms
  • — Travisum can fill in the form for you. Do not hesitate to contact us by email or telephone in this regard.
  • — Below are the different steps to fill in the form (to print, date and sign in black ink).
  • — Start by clicking on the following link for the application: South Sudan Form
  • — Under the heading «Surname», write your surname.
  • — Under the heading «Other names», write your first names.
  • — Under the heading «Nationality», write your nationality.
  • — Under the heading «Sex», indicate your sex.
  • — Under the section «Date of Birth», write your date of birth.
  • — Under the heading «Place of Birth», write your place of birth.
  • — Under the section «Country of residence», indicate your country of residence.
  • — Under the heading «Passport No», write your passport number.
  • — Under the heading «Passeport Type», indicate the type of your passport.
  • — Under the heading «Place of Issue», write the place of issue.
  • — Under the section «Date of Issue», write the date of issue.
  • — Under the section «Date of Expiry», write the expiry date of your passport.
  • — Under the heading «Occupation», indicate your occupation.
  • — Under the heading «Name of the organisation/company», write the name and address of your employer.
  • — Under the heading «Purpose of Visit», indicate the purpose of your trip.
  • — Under the heading «Type of entry», indicate the requested number of entries.
  • — Under the heading «Period of stay», indicate the duration of your stay.
  • — Under the heading «Port of entry», indicate the place of arrival.
  • — Under the heading «Port of departure», indicate the place of departure.
  • — Under the heading «Means of transport», indicate the means of transport for your trip.
  • — Under the section «Currently residing country», write your current address and telephone number.
  • — Under the heading «South Sudan», write the address and phone number of your hotel in South Sudan.
  • — Sign and date your application form.
Proof of financial means
  • — Proof of sufficient financial resources to travel.
Yellow fever vaccination certificate
  • — • If you are not up to date with your vaccinations (vaccination booklet), check with your treating doctor, an approved centre or a hospital. Note that there is a travel clinic (the Travel and Vaccine Clinic of the CHU Saint-Pierre in Brussels) where you can get vaccinated without an appointment.
The return flight reservation
  • — • Print a copy of your return flight reservation.
  • — • The details of all flights are required when it concerns an itinerary (transit, etc.)
Proof of payment
  • — Proof of payment of the consular fees must be provided.

Cover letter from the applicant's employer
  • — • This letter must be written on the company's headed paper.
  • — • State the traveller's name, date of birth and passport number, as well as the name and address of the company.
  • — • Check that the arrival and departure date of the visited country is also stated and specify the desired visa duration and the number of entries.
  • — • It is important to state that the company guarantees all costs related to the trip, with a company stamp and a signature.
  • — • You can download a sample letter of introduction here: Sample letter of introduction
Letter of invitation
  • — • This letter must be written by a company or organisation officially registered in South Sudan.
  • — • State the traveller's name, date of birth and passport number.
  • — • Check that the arrival and departure date of the destination country is also stated, as well as the desired visa duration and the number of entries.
  • — • It is necessary to explain the purpose of the trip and specify the places the traveller will visit.
  • — • Also state that the company guarantees all costs related to the stay in South Sudan.
  • — • The letter must be signed and bear an official company stamp.
  • — • You can download a sample invitation letter here: Sample invitation letter
Processing time
  • — We can handle your application and obtain your visa within 7 working days if you opt for the normal procedure.
